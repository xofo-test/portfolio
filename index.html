About me

Engineering that survives contact with production.

I started in 2016 building Laravel and Angular applications for logistics customers, moved into React and Node platform work, and now spend most of my time on the hardest part of the job: making many systems — APIs, queues, third-party integrators and, increasingly, language models — behave like one reliable product.

As a senior engineer I still write code every day, but my leverage is in decisions. Scope decomposition, API contracts, failure modes, migration plans, and the review culture that keeps a codebase cheap to change. I have led teams of up to six engineers and worked directly with product, design and compliance stakeholders to ship regulated features on schedule.
